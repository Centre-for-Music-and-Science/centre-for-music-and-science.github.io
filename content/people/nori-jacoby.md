Nori Jacoby is Assistant Professor of Psychology at Cornell University and
principal investigator of the Cornell Computational Cognition (CoCoCo) Lab.
His research uses machine learning, large-scale online experiments, and global
fieldwork to study how culture and experience shape perception, with a
particular focus on music cognition.
