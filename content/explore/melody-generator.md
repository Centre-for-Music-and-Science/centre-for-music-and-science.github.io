This app implements a melody generation model, inspired by the work of [David Temperley (2008)](https://davidtemperley.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/temperley-cs08.pdf). By using a Bayesian approach, natural-sounding melodies can be generated by simply sampling from a distribution of pitches.

This demonstrates the role of implicit statistical learning in music perception, showing how a small set of interpretable parameters influence how plausible melodies sound to us as listeners. Adjust these parameters in real time to hear how different statistical assumptions change the resulting melodies.
