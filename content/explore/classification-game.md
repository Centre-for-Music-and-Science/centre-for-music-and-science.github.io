This game presents a series of unfamiliar melodies. Your task is to identify whether they originate from Europe or China. Compete for a top spot on the leaderboard, and test your perception of musical style!

This app helps to deepen our understanding what features of melodies are particularly useful cues for 
determining musical style, and enables us to study how humans make style judgements.
